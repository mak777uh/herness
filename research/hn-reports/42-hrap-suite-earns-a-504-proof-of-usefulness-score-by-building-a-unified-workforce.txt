And why is now the time for it to exist?\n\nHRAP Suite is a workforce operating system that helps organizations manage employees, recruitment, attendance, leave, performance, learning, and workplace operations from a single platform. Now’s a good time for HRAP Suite to exist because many organizations still rely on disconnected HR tools and manual processes that reduce productivity and create administrative overhead.\n\nWhat is your traction to date?
