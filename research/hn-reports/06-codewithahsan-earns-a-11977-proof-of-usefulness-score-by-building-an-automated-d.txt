By leveraging an automated custom Discord bot for mentor matching, CodeWithAhsan delivers real-world educational utility to aspiring developers globally.\n\nWhat does CodeWithAhsan do? And why is now the time for it to exist?\n\nCodeWithAhsan is a developer ecosystem connecting 4,500+ registered members through structured mentorship, collaborative open-source projects, and interactive roadmaps. Supported by a 5,000+ member Discord community, the platform features a custom admin dashboard and a custom Discord bot that automates mentor matching, channel provisioning, and role management. By combining self-paced learning with automated team pairing and interactive AI assistance, it delivers real-world educational utility. Now’s a good time for CodeWithAhsan to exist because there is a growing gap between self-taught programming and the practical, collaborative skills needed to secure modern tech roles.\n\nHow many people does CodeWithAhsan reach?\n\nWe reach approximately 15,000 to 20,000 developers and learners per month across our ecosystem:\n\n\n\n\n• Web Platforms: The main website and blog average 5,000+ unique monthly visitors.\n\n• Active Community: Over 4,500+ registered users on the platform and 5,000+ members on Discord, with hundreds participating daily in mentorship pairings, team chats, and coding challenges.\n\n• Content & Socials: An established YouTube channel (36K+ subscribers), active Twitch streams, GitHub contributions, and social posts generating over 15,000 impressions monthly.\n\nWho does CodeWithAhsan serve? What’s exciting about your users and customers?\n\nOur platform delivers value to two primary groups of developers:\n\nAspiring Developers and Mentees: Junior engineers, self-taught coders, and students who need structured learning roadmaps, peer support, and direct matching with experienced developers to guide their learning and review their code.\nMentors and Project Maintainers: Senior engineers, tech leads, and open-source maintainers looking to give back, publish custom roadmaps, and recruit eager contributors for their collaborative open-source projects.\nNotable Outcomes: Community members have successfully transitioned into professional software engineering roles at global agencies and tech startups, with many remaining active as mentors.\nWhat technologies were used in the making of CodeWithAhsan? And why did you choose the ones most essential to your tech stack?\n\nCodeWithAhsan was built utilizing a robust, modern JavaScript and TypeScript stack featuring Next.js 16 (with App Router and Turbopack) and React 19 to ensure a highly performant, scalable front-end. The user interface is cleanly powered by Tailwind CSS v4 and DaisyUI, while the backend leverages Firebase for fast data and authentication storage paired with Vercel for seamless deployment. Crucially, deep Discord API integrations power the custom bot that handles the complex automation of role management and mentorship matching, serving as the essential connective tissue of the community.\n\nWhat is traction to date for CodeWithAhsan? Around the web, who’s been noticing?\n\nCodeWithAhsan has established an impressive, multi-channel footprint within the developer space. Traction is anchored by a thriving YouTube channel with over 36,000 subscribers, a highly active Discord server, and visible open-source efforts on GitHub. These all seamlessly funnel learners into the main platform ecosystem and its associated tech blog.\n\nCodeWithAhsan scored a 119.77 proof of usefulness score (https://proofofusefulness.com/report/codewithahsan)\n\nWhat excites you about this CodeWithAhsan's potential usefulness?\n\nWhat makes this project incredibly exciting is how it automates the most logistically painful parts of running a developer community:\n\n\n\n\n1. Zero-Friction Mentorship Matching: Instead of manually coordinating pairings, our platform matches mentors and mentees based on interests and automatically provisions temporary, private Discord channels and custom roles for them using our Discord bot.\n\n2. Bridge to Real-world Experience: Mentees don't just consume static tutorials; they apply to participate in real-world collaborative projects listed on the platform, learning git workflows, code reviews, and team dynamics in a guided environment.\n\n3. Integrated Developer Tools: By hosting interactive coding challenges, learning roadmaps, and a client-side AI helper (Logic Buddy), we provide an all-in-one sandbox that takes developers from zero to job-ready, backed by automated admin housekeeping that keeps the community high-quality and self-sustaining.\n\n\n\n\nMeet our sponsors\n\nBright Data: Bright Data is the leading web data infrastructure company, empowering over 20,000 organizations with ethical, scalable access to real-time public web information.
