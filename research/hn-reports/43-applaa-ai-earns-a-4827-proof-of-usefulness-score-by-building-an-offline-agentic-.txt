And why is now the time for it to exist?\n\nApplaa is an Agentic AI learning platform that delivers personalized education for SATs, GCSEs, A-Levels, and major US and UK exams. By combining adaptive AI tutors, real-world app creation, and personalized learning paths, we dramatically reduce tutoring costs, save students time, and improve learning outcomes. With the new school year beginning in September and partnerships with teaching institutions and schools, we expect to grow to more than 5,000 active students per month. Our long-term focus is students aged 14–18 preparing for GCSEs, A-Levels, SATs, AP, IB, and other university entrance exams, with the potential to reach millions of learners across the UK, US, and beyond through our AI-powered personalized learning platform.\n\nWho does Applaa AI serve?\n\nApplaa is for students aged 14–18 preparing for GCSEs, A-Levels, SATs, AP, IB, and other university entrance exams. It is also designed for parents seeking an affordable alternative to expensive private tutoring, which can cost £40–£80 per hour and thousands during exam preparation. Schools, tutoring centres, and teaching institutions use Applaa to deliver personalized AI-powered learning to every student at scale. Today, over 500 active students use the platform each month, with adoption growing through partnerships with schools and teaching institutions.\n\nWhat technologies were used in the making of Applaa AI? And why did you choose the ones most essential to your tech stack?\n\nTo build this ambitious educational tool, Applaa AI leverages Algolia alongside a powerful, custom-built local Agentic AI framework. By designing the application to work completely offline, the team ensures that students have secure and uninterrupted access to their personalized AI tutor without relying on constant internet connectivity.\n\n\n\n\nApplaa AI earned a 48.27 proof of usefulness score (https://proofofusefulness.com/report/applaa-ai)\n\nWhat excites you about this Applaa AI's potential usefulness?\n\nMillions of students spend hundreds of hours preparing for exams, while many parents spend thousands of dollars each month on private tutoring.
