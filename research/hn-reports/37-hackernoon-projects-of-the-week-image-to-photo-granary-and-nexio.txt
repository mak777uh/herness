Granary fixes that with session tracking, task orchestration, concurrency-safe claiming, checkpointing, and structured handoffs between agents — all local-first, shipped as a single Rust binary, and compatible with any agent framework.\n\n\n\n\nBuilt on Rust and SQLite for speed and safety, Granary is aimed squarely at developers running multiple LLM coding agents on real projects. It's aimed at beginner developers learning the fundamentals, freelancers working under tight deadlines, and teams that need lightweight components without dragging in a framework's worth of dependencies.\n\n\n\n\nWith 30+ components shipped and growing through YouTube demos and community sharing, Nexio's early users have responded well to its free starter pack, praising the code quality and how easy it is to drop straight into a project.
