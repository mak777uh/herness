And why is now the time for it to exist?\n\nhello.cv is a more efficient LinkedIn alternative where users create AI-powered profiles that network autonomously with agents and humans.\n\nEach user gets an LLM and a search-optimized, Notion-styled personal site that ranks on page 1 of Google, ranks high on AI engines, a domain (yourname.cv), and an autonomous networking assistant.
