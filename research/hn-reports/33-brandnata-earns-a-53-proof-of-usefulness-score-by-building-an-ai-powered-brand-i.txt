Whether you’re a solopreneur, part of an early-stage startup, or a developer building something that truly matters, the Proof of Usefulness Hackathon is your chance to test your product’s utility, get featured on HackerNoon, and compete for $150k+ in prizes. Submit your project to get started!\n\n\n\n\nIn this interview, we speak with Gaurav Pareek about BrandNata, an AI-powered brand intelligence platform built for enterprise marketing teams. BrandNata helps organizations monitor brand performance, analyze sentiment, track campaigns across hundreds of entities, and make faster, data-driven marketing decisions through a unified analytics dashboard.\n\nWhat does BrandNata do?\n\nBrandNata is an AI-powered brand intelligence platform that enables enterprises to track, measure, and optimize marketing campaigns across hundreds of entities from a single dashboard. It combines real-time brand monitoring, sentiment analysis, campaign performance tracking, audience intelligence, and AI-driven recommendations to help marketing teams improve brand performance and maximize ROI.\n\n\n\n\nNow is the right time for BrandNata because marketing teams manage campaigns across multiple channels, brands, and regions while dealing with enormous volumes of fragmented data. Businesses need a centralized platform that transforms this information into actionable insights, allowing teams to make faster decisions, protect brand reputation, and optimize marketing investments in real time.\n\nWhat is your traction to date?\n\nBrandNata is an enterprise-focused platform designed for organizations managing large-scale marketing operations. The platform enables businesses to monitor more than 350 entities through a unified analytics dashboard while expanding its reach through enterprise partnerships, digital marketing, and product-led adoption. As BrandNata continues to grow, its goal is to serve thousands of marketing professionals and enterprise teams worldwide.\n\nWho does BrandNata serve?\n\nBrandNata is built for enterprise marketing teams, brand managers, marketing leaders, agencies, and organizations managing multiple brands, campaigns, or business units. It helps teams centralize campaign analytics, monitor brand reputation, understand audience sentiment, optimize marketing performance, and make better strategic decisions using AI-powered insights.\n\n\n\n\nWhat excites us most is seeing organizations replace fragmented reporting across multiple tools with one unified source of truth that delivers real-time visibility into brand health and campaign performance.\n\nWhat technologies were used in the making of BrandNata? And why did you choose the ones most essential to your tech stack?\n\nBrandNata is built using Next.js, React, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, Docker, and AWS Cloud to provide a scalable enterprise platform. Its AI capabilities leverage Large Language Models (LLMs), Vector Databases, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, and LangChain to deliver contextual brand intelligence, sentiment analysis, campaign optimization, and actionable recommendations.\n\n\n\n\nThese technologies were selected to deliver real-time analytics, enterprise-grade scalability, and intelligent automation while processing data from multiple digital channels efficiently.\n\nBrandNata scored a 53 Proof of Usefulness Score (https://proofofusefulness.com/reports/brandnata). How do you feel about that? Needs reassessment or just right?\n\nWe're encouraged by the 53 Proof of Usefulness Score because it validates the problem we're solving and the direction we're taking. While we believe the score reflects the platform well today, we also see it as motivation to continue improving. As we onboard more enterprise customers, expand integrations, and introduce additional AI-powered capabilities, we expect BrandNata's usefulness and real-world impact to grow even further.\n\nWhat excites you about BrandNata's potential usefulness?\n\nBrandNata helps organizations move beyond basic reporting by delivering real-time brand intelligence powered by AI. Instead of manually combining data from multiple platforms, marketing teams receive unified insights, sentiment analysis, campaign recommendations, and audience intelligence in one place. We believe this enables faster decision-making, stronger brand performance, and more effective marketing investments across the enterprise.\n\nWalk us through your most concrete evidence of usefulness. Not vanity metrics or projections — what's the one data point that proves people genuinely need what you've built?\n\nThe strongest evidence is that marketing teams can replace multiple disconnected reporting tools with a single AI-powered dashboard that tracks campaigns, brand mentions, sentiment, and performance across hundreds of entities. Instead of manually gathering data from different platforms, teams receive centralized insights and recommendations that help them make faster, more informed marketing decisions.\n\nHow do you measure genuine user adoption versus \"tourists\" who sign up but never return? What's your retention story?\n\nWe focus on product engagement rather than sign-up numbers. We monitor how frequently teams return to review dashboards, analyze campaign performance, monitor brand sentiment, and use AI-generated recommendations during active marketing campaigns. Our objective is to become part of a team's daily decision-making process, where BrandNata is used continuously throughout the campaign lifecycle rather than only during reporting periods.\n\nIf we re-score your project in 12 months, which criterion will show the biggest improvement, and what are you doing right now to make that happen?\n\nWe expect our adoption and real-world impact scores to improve the most. We're actively expanding platform integrations, enhancing AI-powered analytics, improving recommendation accuracy, and working closely with enterprise marketing teams to refine workflows based on customer feedback. Our goal is to make BrandNata the central intelligence platform for enterprise marketing operations.\n\nHow Did You Hear About HackerNoon? Share With Us About Your Experience With HackerNoon.\n\nWe've followed HackerNoon for years as one of the best platforms for technology professionals, startups, and developers to discover innovative products and share practical knowledge. The Proof of Usefulness initiative stood out because it emphasizes solving real customer problems rather than focusing only on funding or marketing, making it a valuable opportunity to showcase BrandNata.\n\nWith your presence across enterprise AI content and industry publications, how are these media appearances converting into active, engaged users?\n\nMedia coverage helps us reach marketing leaders, enterprise decision-makers, and technology professionals who are actively looking for AI-powered marketing solutions. Rather than measuring success only by traffic, we focus on qualified demo requests, product conversations, and long-term relationships with organizations that see BrandNata as part of their marketing technology stack.\n\nWith your current focus on enterprise adoption and product-led growth, what specific milestones are you targeting to scale BrandNata over the next year?\n\nOver the next 12 months, we're focused on expanding enterprise adoption, adding more integrations with major marketing platforms, enhancing AI-driven recommendations, and strengthening our analytics capabilities. We also plan to improve automation, broaden our channel coverage, and continue building features that help organizations measure brand performance more accurately and efficiently.\n\nBrandNata uses AI-powered analytics, sentiment analysis, and campaign intelligence to improve marketing decisions. Can you share a specific example where the platform delivered insights that traditional reporting tools would have missed?\n\nOne example is identifying negative shifts in brand sentiment across multiple platforms while a campaign is still running. Traditional reporting tools often provide historical performance data after a campaign ends, but BrandNata combines real-time sentiment analysis, campaign metrics, and audience signals to alert marketing teams early. This allows them to adjust messaging, reallocate budgets, or respond to emerging issues before they significantly affect campaign performance or brand reputation.\n\n\n\n\nMeet our sponsors\n\nBright Data: Bright Data is the leading web data infrastructure company, empowering over 20,000 organizations with ethical, scalable access to real-time public web information.
