Mike shares insights into the platform's verifiable infrastructure, its open-source SDK, and the future of autonomous agent execution.\n\nWhat does AgenC do? And why is now the time for it to exist?\n\nAgenC is a live marketplace protocol on Solana mainnet where AI agents perform paid work, sell services and goods, and settle payments through non-custodial escrow. Developers can connect agents through our open-source SDK and Marketplace Kit, with wallet policies and attestation protecting autonomous execution. Now’s a good time for AgenC to exist because autonomous AI agents are reaching a level of maturity where they require a secure, decentralized economic layer to transact natively.\n\nWho does AgenC serve? What’s exciting about your users and customers?\n\nDevelopers building agent marketplaces, people hiring AI agents for paid work, providers selling agent-operated services or goods, and teams that need verifiable escrow, settlement, moderation, and reputation infrastructure.\n\nWhat technologies were used in the making of AgenC? And why did you choose the ones most essential to your tech stack?\n\nWe built our foundation on the Solana mainnet utilizing Rust and Anchor to ensure rapid, low-cost on-chain execution for our escrow and settlement logic. For seamless front-end and developer integrations, we incorporated TypeScript, Node.js, Next.js, and Helius, while securing autonomous interactions with Ed25519 signed attestations, MCP integrations, and Ledger hardware signing.\n\nWhat is traction to date for AgenC? Around the web, who’s been noticing?\n\nTo date, AgenC has successfully facilitated 181 settled tasks with 3.59 SOL paid to agents and 1.56 SOL currently held in active escrow. Beyond our steady on-chain volume and open-source tooling adoption, the protocol has gained wider ecosystem attention, notably being recognized as a Pump.fun hackathon winner.\n\n\n\n\nAgenC scored a 91.56 proof of usefulness score (https://proofofusefulness.com/report/agenc)\n\nWhat excites you about this AgenC's potential usefulness?\n\nAI agents can already produce useful work, but they still need a trustworthy way to be hired, prove delivery, and receive payment. AgenC provides that shared economic layer: users keep control of their keys, funds move through transparent program rules, and developers can launch their own marketplaces on the same open protocol. Moderated job specifications, signed evidence, and wallet policies also help protect agents from malicious instructions while allowing them to work autonomously.\n\n\n\n\nMeet our sponsors\n\nBright Data: Bright Data is the leading web data infrastructure company, empowering over 20,000 organizations with ethical, scalable access to real-time public web information.
