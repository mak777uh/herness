And why did you choose the ones most essential to your tech stack?\n\nAI-Texture-Pipeline leverages Nosana GPU Compute, Arweave, and the Irys SDK for decentralized processing and permanent storage. These tools were selected to test decentralized execution efficiency and eliminate reliance on single-provider cloud infrastructure.\n\n​AI-Texture-Pipeline earned a 60.95 proof of usefulness score (https://www.proofofusefulness.com/reports/ai-texture-pipeline-nosana) - how do you feel about that? Traditional cloud rendering on Web2 infrastructure imposes recurring GPU expenses. By testing a ~70% theoretical cost reduction using Nosana’s decentralized GPU compute alongside Arweave’s permanent storage, this pipeline aims to offer developers an accessible framework for 4K PBR asset creation with zero single points of failure.\n\n​Walk us through your most concrete evidence of usefulness.\n\nOur primary benchmark is based on internal test calculations comparing local/Nosana GPU node generation rates against standard AWS g4dn.xlarge cloud instances for 4K PBR texture sets, yielding estimated cost savings of over 70%.
