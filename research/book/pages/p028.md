KRISHNAMURTI: Therefore thought is always wo rking in a prison. It can call it  freedom, it can call it beauty, it can call it  what it likes! But it is always within  the limitations of the barbed  wire fence. Now I want to find out whether thought  has any place except in ther e. Thought has no place wh en I say, “I don’t know.” 
“I really don’t know.” Right? 
 
Needleman: For the moment. 
 
KRISHNAMURTI: I really don’t know. I only know this, a nd I really don’t know  whether thought can function in any field at all, excep t this. I really don’t know. 
When I say, “I don’t know”, which doesn’t mean I am expecting to know, when I  say I really don’t know—what happens? I climb down the ladder. I become, the  mind becomes, completely humble. 
Now that state of “not knowing” is intelligence. Then it can operate in the  field of the known and be free to work somewhere else if it wants to.
