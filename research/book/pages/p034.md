KRISHNAMURTI: Then there is something else  that will operate, which will  function within the field of the known. But without finding that merely to say... 
 
Needleman: No, no, this is so. 
 
KRISHNAMURTI: Let’s proceed. Space is betw een two thoughts, between two  factors of time, two periods of time, because thought is time. Yes?

KRISHNAMURTI: You can have a dozen periods of time but it is still thought,  there is that space. Then there is the space round the centre, and the space beyond  the self, beyond the barb ed wire, beyond the wall of the centre. KRISHNAMURTI: All that is manufactured by the centre. To speculate about what  is beyond all that has no meaning to  me personally, it’s the philosopher’s  amusement.
