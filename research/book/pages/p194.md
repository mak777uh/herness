I have been made dull by my wife,  by the repetitions of the blasted sacred books, by the society in which I live, I  have become completely indifferent. So, what shall I do? I recognize that as a fact. And the  question is not how to get rid of it! I don’t condemn it. I say, “That is a fact.” So  what shall I do? Well, Sirs. What shall I do?
