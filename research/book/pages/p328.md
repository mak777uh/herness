There are two problems involved, aren’t ther e? There is the scar of pain, of hurt,  and the knowledge of it makes me afraid  of tomorrow. Can the mind be free of  that scar? Now let’s examine that. 
I am sure most of us have some kind  of psychological scars. Haven’t you?— of course. We are not talking about the physical scars which affect the brain—we  can leave that aside for th e moment. There are the psychological scars of hurt. 
How is the mind, the brain, to be free of them? Must it be free of them? Is not the  memory of being hurt a protection agains t the future? Verba lly, in many ways  you have hurt me; there is a memory of it. If I forget that, I come innocently to  you next morning and you hurt  me again. So what am I to do? Think it out, Sirs,  go on.
