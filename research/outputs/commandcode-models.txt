недоступны:

|---|---|---|---|
| stealth/crouton-alpha | Crouton Alpha | 1 048 576 | /chat/completions |
| stealth/glyph-cluster:free | Glyph Cluster | 256 000 | /chat/completions, /responses |
| reflection/beam:free | Beam 1.0 | 1 048 576 | /chat/completions, /responses |

deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro, moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro и другие.
