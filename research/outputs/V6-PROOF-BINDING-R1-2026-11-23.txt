Implementation
--------------
- bin/chain.py accepts only closed proof objects {kind,artifact,sha256,subject_sha256}.
- kind v1: lean-instance. Digests: 64 lowercase hex, bare form.
- subject: SHA-256 of strict canonical JSON (repository JCS subset) for the whole chain after removing only bridge-level `proven` from adjacent/downstream.
- artifact: repo-relative POSIX path; absolute, drive-qualified, backslash, empty/dot/parent segments and resolved escape are refused before read.
- exact artifact bytes are hashed; proof truth is not inferred from the hash.
- strict CLI refuses duplicate keys, float, NaN and integers outside ±(2^53−1).
- tests/test_chain.py is now part of `hs selftest`.
- proofs/Proofs/ChainInstances.lean models concrete proof-presence and method-coverage instances.
- bin/chain_report.py regenerates research/proofs/k9-chain-instances.json deterministically.

Residual / what this does not prove
------------------------------------
- The checker proves binding, not truth: a matching digest does not establish that a theorem is valid.
- The checker does not run pinned Lean; proof-package regeneration belongs to the later admission layer.
- Lean models proof presence and policy semantics; digest strings are opaque there.
- Repo-relative resolution is checked, but symlink/TOCTOU races inside the repository are outside this slice.
- Downstream code, hidden channels and Rust/Lean correspondence remain unverified.
- This is K9_model_checked, not K9_proven. No owner key was created; active remains empty.
