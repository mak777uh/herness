<p>In the early morning of October 21, LiteLLM's release pipeline was compromised
and pushed v1.107.0 to the docker registry and PyPi. This version contained
compiled malware which steals credentials and loads remote-code execution tools.
<li>A malicious <code>litellm==1.107.0</code> package was published to PyPI and was live for approximately 2 hours (11:39 UTC to 13:23 UTC). <strong>It has since been pulled.</strong></li>
